Safety principles

  • Exact calculated dose is never replaced by the rounded recommendation.
  • Clinical hard limits are applied before operational rounding.
  • Unknown or incomplete calculation rules block the dose instead of guessing.
  • Carboplatin kidney function is explicit; no hidden universal 125 mL/min cap.
  • IU remains IU. The engine never silently treats Bleomycin units as mg.
  • No patient inputs are stored in localStorage.

Safety / deployment notes

This completed production release contains six pilot regimens with local approval recorded per project-owner confirmation on 7 October 2026. Future regimens still require the same structured validation and approval workflow before publication. Rounding policy is editable locally and exportable for governed central publication.

Data governance

Clinical calculations use structured dose objects only. Free-text legacy doses are never fed to the calculation engine. Draft regimen data may be stored in browser localStorage; patient inputs are not persisted.

Recommended production promotion gate
